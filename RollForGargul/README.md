# RollFor - Gargul

Lets Gargul users in your raid see what [RollFor](https://github.com/rollfor/rollfor) is
doing:

- Rolls you start show up in their Gargul window.
- Your soft-res data is sent to them when you import, and when Gargul asks for it.

## Requirements

`RollFor`.

## Options

On/off switch on its page in RollFor's options window. Nothing to configure.
